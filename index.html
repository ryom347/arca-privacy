# Arca Privacy Policy Last updated: October 4, 2026 Developer: [Developer name displayed on Google Play] This policy explains how the Android application “Arca” handles information. ## 1. Information Arca Handles Arca accesses photos and videos you have authorized it to access, along with associated information such as file names, capture dates, and file sizes, to provide backup, lightweight copy creation, viewing, and management features. Original files may contain metadata, including the location where a photo or video was taken. Arca also stores connection information for your chosen backup destination, including its address, shared folder, username, and password, as well as management information such as synchronization history, settings, and favorites. ## 2. Storage and Data Transmission Original photos and videos, along with information needed to manage backups, are transferred to the drive or SMB server you configure. Lightweight copies, thumbnails, settings, and other local management information are stored on your device. Arca does not send your photos or videos to servers operated by the developer, and the developer does not collect your photos or videos. Arca does not use a developer-provided cloud service for backups. ## 3. Permissions Photo and video permissions are used for backup and viewing. Camera access is used for the photography feature. Network and Wi-Fi information are used to check connectivity and perform synchronization. Notifications and background execution capabilities support synchronization and communicate its progress and results. Owner authentication uses Android’s authentication features. Arca does not access or store fingerprint data or facial biometric authentication data. ## 4. Sharing and Third-Party Services When you choose to share a photo or video or open it in another application, Arca provides the selected content to the application you specify. Subsequent handling is governed by that application or service’s policies. Arca does not include advertising or usage analytics SDKs. Your information is not sold. ## 5. Information Protection Backup destination connection settings are encrypted on your device using Android Keystore. The Locked Album restricts access to viewing copies managed by Arca on your device. It does not encrypt or protect originals on your backup drive or copies stored elsewhere. ## 6. Retention and Deletion Information is retained for as long as necessary to provide the app’s features and maintain synchronization records. You can delete settings and local copies through the app’s available features or Android settings. Clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling it removes settings, management records, and other data stored in the app’s private storage. However, photos and videos in shared device storage, originals on your backup drive, and copies shared with other applications are not automatically deleted. These must be deleted from their respective locations. To free up device storage, original files are removed only after the backup and lightweight copies have been verified and any required confirmations have been completed. ## 7. Contact For questions about this policy, please contact: Email: [Public contact email address] Information you provide when contacting the developer is used to respond to your inquiry and retained for as long as necessary to handle it. ## 8. Changes to This Policy This page will be updated if the app’s features or information handling practices change. Significant changes will also be communicated within the app where necessary.
